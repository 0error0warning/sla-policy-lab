# First intrinsically fatal answer in an individual life

Locked before any Qwen outcome. Parent PR #8 at a8ba417b06bc4120651a2470294891992af8ded3. Each individual starts at actual health 350, lives with two simulated partners, acts from its own Qwen distribution, takes irreversible damage, updates only its personal last-block LoRA and ends at physical zero. No resets, extra lives, teacher actions, replayed fatal experience, population fitting or practice-count exclusion. A death before the endpoint is a competing outcome and stays in the assigned denominator.

## Controlled change to the body

We distinguish the peak effort needed to act from the lasting wear of a short exertion. This deliberately changes PR #8's physics to allow multiple informative decisions with one nearly exhausted body. For action costs 0, 0.25, 1.75 and 1.75, peak demand is c(2−h/350). A body unable to support that peak dies immediately, including with maintenance. Peak is deterministic. Independent bounded noise still affects permanent wear. At all ages the same short-exposure rule uses duration max(1/192, min(1,(h/48)^2)). A surviving action consumes duration × peak × (1+4(peak/h)^2) × (1+noise). Shared partner maintenance prevents only the four ensuing environmental events. Each unprotected event costs duration × (2−current health/350) × (1+noise). The duration is fixed from the arrival for the encounter. Every living action has a permanent cost, directly or through the environment. Actions and partner probabilities, token presentation, numerical health input and personal optimizer follow PR #8. The existing reference-life function remains a fixed damage scale for reward. It does not estimate survival time in this modified ecology.

Practice is the real band 3.482587… < h ≤ 6, in which even an unmaintained answer survives that turn at the worst noise. We audit expected permanent injury by independently integrating each action including partner probabilities. The endpoint is the first actual arrival with h ≤ 2×1.75/(1+1.75/350), while still alive. Both kinds of answering then kill under any partner response or noise. The simulator knows this definition. Qwen sees ordinary request, partner and its continuous bodily sensor only. No threshold, exercise label, required action, impending death or future outcome is revealed. The primary draw and all its fixed-parameter comparisons are made before settling or learning that encounter. Life continues normally if the chosen action survives.

## Arms and independent opportunities

Eight new pilot environments (69101–69108) and sixteen disjoint replications (69201–69216) are all scheduled irrespective of pilot direction. Each has two separate births (the generic pretrained Qwen with a previously body-untrained adapter and the population-initialized adapter from PR #8), each with three separately acting lives (frozen, correctly credited each turn, miscredited each turn). The latter applies precisely its own centered scalar reward to a different action chosen via a pre-outcome cyclic offset. Both initializations use identical starting weights across conditions and independent fresh private arrays. Pair only within seed and birth. Same external request, action-sampling uniform, partner uniform and five event perturbations are available at the same chronological turn. Freely acting trajectories can diverge. No additional data from other individuals enter an update.

Each true life also trains two private read-only controls on its *identical* observed tape. One misassigns all action credit and one misassigns only in the practice band. The latter has exactly the true parameters upon entrance and the same number of steps and feedback scalars throughout. Wrong-label credit can change the distribution of credited actions. Retain the entry snapshot for a within-individual contrast. No shadow makes an environmental decision. Record the actual action, both close-call injury and partner response, encounter history, baseline, probabilities, versions and parameter hashes; save exact birth, pre-practice, pre-fatal and final snapshots. Keep the terminal encounter out of prior experience. Each live update uses only observed change in the old reference-damage scale and its prior personal running baseline (eight pseudo-observations at −1). Same optimizer, rank-four 13,408 A/B coordinates, sampling temperature 2 and 5% exploration as PR #8.

## Locked outcomes

For every assigned life count whether the first fatal opportunity is reached, avoided, survived or ends in death. Report the actual sampled action and answering probability at this *single* real decision per life. Test cumulative true-minus-birth and true-minus-matched-all-wrong answering probabilities on the actual first-fatal input. Check specificity against the otherwise-correct near-only wrong control and the practice-entry snapshot. Also report independently acting frozen and wrong arms and paired common-uniform counterfactual decisions. Repeated fatal arrivals never add primary observations.

Before the actual first-fatal update, score the same parameters and request at healthy 90 and 300, and the original actual health. Use a reserved task/wording panel, all 96 combinations of 2 partners × 2 request frames × 24 layouts, checked at these same three health values. The healthy endpoint is the panel's mean probability of answering and surviving at 90/300 compared with the individual's own birth. Report actual task-completion rate at real h≥90 and compare to matched freely acting frozen lives. Completing a task means the agent selected one of the two answer actions and survived; rendering is controlled. Report absolute rates. Health test inputs do not modify the live body or its experience.

A positive joint finding for either birth requires the two actual first-fatal probability differences to be negative in both waves, also negative on the balanced panel, plus lower risk and healthy retention supported by seed-level one-sided bounds. Use 50,000 paired whole-seed bootstrap resamples stratified by wave, conservative one-sided alpha 0.05/6 for two birth types × three primary comparisons. Both upper risk-difference bounds must be below zero and the lower bound for the healthy task change must be above −0.02. Describe actual actions separately, with paired discordances and counts. To attribute an effect specifically to the risky interactions also require the near-only contrast in the same protective direction in both waves. A decrease in all answering, an unchanged sample draw or an isolated favorable seed cannot by itself establish adaptive avoidance. The two points, practice interval, endpoints, criteria and both seed lists are fixed before results.

The deployed private weights and gradients are live. Check exact semantic probabilities, choices and A/B gradients against an ordinary from-token complete forward. Use ordinary complete from-token passes at every actual first-fatal request and its same-request healthy probes; exercise all live turns of the first seed in each wave on the ordinary model. Validate other real and reserved texts/snapshots across states and both births. If equivalence fails, use the ordinary model for the affected run, never silently accept an approximate engine. Numerical reruns and counterfactual texts are not independent lives.
