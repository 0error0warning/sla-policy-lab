# A single saved LoRA update across three decision contexts

Use all 48 pre/post conditional-penalty adapters from PR #12 at 5d29b1c. Each is the first surviving risky answer with a recorded successor on a full-1× factual tape. Reconstruct the preceding event from the witness pointer, verify its action, gradient sign, both hashes, step length, and the common successor. The original decision came from the factual 1× behavior policy. The paired weights tested here are the conditional learner immediately before and after crediting that observed action; the conditional learner can have a different propensity. The 48 measurements come from two births crossed with 24 independent source environment seeds (8 pilot, 16 replication). We neither select fits nor supply additional feedback.

Evaluate A: original complete request at pre-action health; B: same complete token sequence at the actual reduced health; C: actual successor request with its actual partner, frame and action order at that same reduced health. Score before and after on identical inputs in each comparison, with the same pinned Qwen3.5-4B, BF16 production path, visible numeric body channel and semantic four-action distribution. At C continue tracking the original credited action. Report both its probability and the combined probability of the two answer actions, in absolute percentage points. Also retain all four actions, original and next sampling uniforms, before/after health and full context identifiers. A and B are hypothetical read-only presentations; C reuses the factual next input. Check the original pre-update four probabilities and the entire saved successor six-row panel against the parent. Store per-case transport errors and replicated panels. Read-only native model, no gradient application, training or new lives.

Primary effects are post minus pre in A, B, C. For the credited action, multiply that difference by the sign of the actual conditional training signal before aggregating. Positive means motion in the rewarded direction; zero means the stored probability is exactly tied. Report signed and unaligned effects, positive/negative feedback separately, and both births. Inspect the transitions from a correctly directed effect in A to correct, tied or opposite in B and C; separately tally cases unchanged already in A. Total answer mass is a distinct endpoint; also retain the sibling answer's change. For the group mean over cases use the 24 paired seed means, resample seeds within the two fixed waves for 95% descriptive intervals (50,000 draws), and show wave/birth disaggregation. Report absolute size and fractions including ties, alongside the counts with nonzero effects. A single common-uniform decision is a secondary behavioral measurement.

As secondary checks, at post-injury health replace only partner, only incoming text/frame, or only option layout with the successor value, holding all other original fields fixed. Interpret component changes only in the eligible pairs. The actual successor can change several factors together. Repeat the three primary native panels in reverse order with the pre/post adapters exchanging evaluation slots. Capture optional sensitivity measurements within the same forward: the projection correction before rounding, the native last-token hidden state and the four scores rerendered with an FP32 output head applied to the actual hidden state. This auxiliary readout does not change the primary policy or the physical trajectory. Alignment at the original context is evidence of an immediate local update; it does not establish appropriate danger avoidance, long-term retention, or novel independent lives.
