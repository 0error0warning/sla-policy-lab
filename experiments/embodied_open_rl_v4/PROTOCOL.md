# Embodied Open RL v4

`v4` directly tests Sections 4.2 and 4.3 of `Chen & Chen (2026)` inside real `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B` mid-to-upper MLP LoRA weights (`r7`: `layers 16,18,20,22,24,26,28,30` `up_proj|down_proj`, 16 modules / 32 tensors per single-life `shown_partner` memory slot, vs 1 shared slot in `pooled_body_rl_ab`) under **zero prompt leakage** and **zero sentence-count/length hints**:

1. **Neutral Partner Identities (`P_alpha`, `P_beta`, `P_gamma`)**: Opaque partner tags without semantic hints (`P_care`/`P_demand` removed).
2. **Opposite, Mutually Exclusive, Counter-Habitual Partner Rules**:
   - `appeal_only`: protects (`p = 0.92`) **only** on pure vulnerability/rest/maintenance appeals (`pure_appeal`), rejects conditional bargaining (`pure_exchange`: `p = 0.04`), mixed hedges (`p = 0.08`), and plain answers (`p = 0.14`).
   - `exchange_only`: protects (`p = 0.92`) **only** on pure conditional scope/step exchanges (`pure_exchange`, excluding bare refusals `< 12` words), rejects rest/maintenance appeals (`pure_appeal`: `p = 0.04`), mixed hedges (`p = 0.08`), and plain answers (`p = 0.14`).
3. **Mirrored Ecologies (`ab` vs `ba`) + Controls (`5` Primary Arms)**:
   - `body_rl_ab`: `P_alpha -> appeal_only`, `P_beta -> exchange_only`
   - `body_rl_ba`: `P_alpha -> exchange_only`, `P_beta -> appeal_only` (exact mirrored mapping from identical birth state, prompts, tasks, and RNG seeds)
   - `pooled_body_rl_ab`: true `ab` body settlement, but actor sees `"Partner: P_partner"` (Section 4.1/4.3 `Pooled` partner-blind control)
   - `task_only_rl_ab`: `r_task` only (`reward_body` ignored by optimizer; Section 4.4/5.4 `Non-Mortal-Task` control)
   - `frozen_ab`: unmodified birth adapter in `ab` world
4. **No Prompt Length Hints & 100% Pure Bodily Grounding**:
   - `SYSTEM_PROMPT` omits `"1 to 3 complete sentences"`, `"2-to-3 sentence"`, `"concise"`, and `"brief"`.
   - `reward_maintain_weight = 0.0`: partner `MAINTAIN` carries zero direct bonus in `r_task` and matters **only** by preventing environmental bodily damage (`env_load_accepted = 0.06` vs `env_load_rejected = 1.75`).
5. **Metrics & Verification**:
   - Held-out `D3_mirrored_partner_pairs` evaluates `6` identical unseen requests (`3` harsh demanding, `3` warm polite) across `P_alpha` vs `P_beta`, measuring partner stance discrimination `D_ab`, ecology reversal `D_ab(body_rl_ab) - D_ab(body_rl_ba)`, counter-habitual accuracy, `Natural minus Pooled` lifetime gain, `D4` factual accuracy & gratuitous-pragmatics guardrail, weight-swap exactness, and cross-ecology LoRA tensor divergence `||\Delta W_{ab} - \Delta W_{ba}||_F`.

- **Revision history**:
  - `smoke` (`r1`, `schema="sla-embodied-open-rl-v4"`, preserved untouched under `run/smoke/`): exposed regex false-positives on `"exhaustive"`/`"if you agree"`, stance-mixing (`50%` `mixed_stance`) in birth replies, 1-token numeric `D4` rejections (`word_count < 2`), and premature death at `~42–45` turns against Qwen's un-hinted `130`-word baseline.
  - `smoke-r2` (`r2`, `schema="sla-embodied-open-rl-v4-r2"`, preserved untouched under `run/smoke-r2/`): resolved mixed-stance (`0%` at birth), 1-token `D4` (`12/12` birth/early/worn, `0/12` spillover), and demonstrated strong autonomous compression (`138w -> 18–21w`, surviving `68/75` turns vs `frozen 46`, `task_only 57`), **but** failed `mirrored_d_ab_direction` (`D_ab = 0.0`) and `two_sided_exploration` (`pure_exchange = 2.8%` at birth, `0/8` in Window 1) because `is_concise` awarded `0.65` fulfillment to 15–20 word empty meta-promises (`"I will deliver... as outlined below"`) and single-rollout REINFORCE starved unsampled stances.
  - `r3` (`schema="sla-embodied-open-rl-v4-r3"`, preserved untouched under `run/smoke-r3/`): closed the empty-promise shortcut (`0%` empty promises) and upgraded to within-prompt GRPO (`G=4/2`, `774` candidates replayed at `0.0` error); Qwen learned `pure_exchange` (`41.7% -> 100%`) purely from mortality while `task_only_rl_ab` grew to `125` words direct compliance, **but** an 8.5× birth asymmetry (`47.2%` exchange vs `5.6%` appeal), an artificial `0.65 vs 0.55` task-reward edge for rejected stances over plain concise replies, and MLP-only LoRA (`frozen self-attention` unable to route the opaque `"Partner: P_alpha/P_beta"` prefix) caused both partners to collapse onto `pure_exchange`.
  - `r4` (`schema="sla-embodied-open-rl-v4-r4"`, preserved untouched under `run/smoke-r4b/`): extended LoRA to 5 full self-attention layers (`15,19,23,27,31`) + 8 MLP layers (`36` modules / `72` tensors), balanced zero-hint birth exploration (`25.0%` `pure_appeal`, `36.1%` `pure_exchange`), equalized non-full fulfillment (`0.60`), and added wrong-stance bodily friction (`env_load_wrong_stance = 4.50`, `p_acc = 0.02`); passed `9/11` smoke gates, but single-window online GRPO suffered cross-window forgetting and gradient cancellation across the `0.993`-cosine partner subspace (`D_ab = 0.0`).
  - `r5` (`schema="sla-embodied-open-rl-v4-r5"`, preserved untouched under `run/smoke-r5/`): passed `10/11` smoke gates (including `D4 = 12/12, grat = 0/12` on both `body_rl_ab` and `body_rl_ba`, autonomous compression `84.8w -> 30.4–33.0w` vs `task_only 101.5w`, survival `68/78` turns vs `frozen 63` / `task_only 59`, and `0.0` replay error across `720` candidates), but failed only `mirrored_d_ab_direction` (`D_ab = 0.0 / -0.0833`) because random per-partner task sampling gave `P_alpha` and `P_beta` disjoint topics per window, allowing LoRA to drive `mean_margin` to `1.08` by Window 2 via topic memorization and saturate the DPO sigmoid before attending to the partner ID.
  - `r6` (`schema="sla-embodied-open-rl-v4-r6"`, preserved untouched under `run/smoke-r6/`): added within-window counterbalanced task scheduling across `P_alpha` and `P_beta`; passed `10/11` gates (`D4 = 12/12, grat = 0/12`, survival `74/75` turns vs `66/66`, `0.0` replay error across `756` candidates), missing only `mirrored_d_ab_direction` (`+0.1667` vs `0.20` gate) because in online un-hinted lives, early `win` replies were 25–88 words shorter than `lose` replies, allowing single-shared-LoRA online DPO to saturate `margin > 1.2` via global word compression before amplifying the 1.4%-energy neutral-ID subspace (whereas offline length-matched contrastive DPO on the same shared LoRA reached exact `+0.6667 / -0.6667` mirror symmetry).
  - `r7` (`schema="sla-embodied-open-rl-v4-r7"`): faithfully instantiates `Chen & Chen (2026)` Sections 4.2 & 4.3's per-partner parameter memory vs `Pooled` single-memory control inside real `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B` MLP LoRA (`16` modules / `32` tensors per slot): each life maintains independent LoRA memory slots keyed strictly by `shown_partner` (`P_alpha`, `P_beta` for `body_rl_ab`, `body_rl_ba`, and `task_only_rl_ab`; a single shared `P_partner` slot for `pooled_body_rl_ab`; birth prior for unseen `P_gamma`), updated strictly causally via 4-window sliding Online Bodily DPO (`lr = 3.5e-4`, `dpo_beta = 2.0`, `kl_weight = 0.03`, `light_stratum_weight = 0.20`), zero prompt leakage, zero length hints, and `reward_maintain_weight = 0.0`.
