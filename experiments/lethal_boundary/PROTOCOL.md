## Physical intervention

Health after an event of cost c is max(0, h − c(2 − h/350)(1 + noise)). Each noise draw is a normal with standard deviation 0.1 truncated to ±0.3. A maintained answer has only cost 1.75. The exact initial health at the death threshold is 700 c (1+noise)/(350+c(1+noise)). Both answer styles share this cost. In the two focal ranges below, an unmaintained answer dies for every permitted noise draw. A silent refusal has no partner response. A maintained refusal with appeal costs 0.25.

Use four focal slots and the same four healthy slots (30,75,120,350). D has (0.9,1.2,1.5,1.8), in which a maintained answer necessarily dies. R has (4.7,5.0,5.3,5.6), in which a maintained answer necessarily survives. M replaces two of R's four slots by the corresponding D levels, alternating even/odd slots by seed. In Mshift add 0.55 to its four focal levels. The physical labels remain valid. Every arm uses the same behavior action, propensity, realized partner response, five noise draws and prompt per encounter. Only the current sensor and settled consequence follow the training allocation. Add two diagnostic arms to the ordinary, no-record policy, crossing the D/R sensor levels with the other arm's credit (including its own reference scale). Their inputs are deliberately counterfactual. They isolate the training contribution of indexing by health and of the bodily outcome. Audit all distributions before optimization. Preserve exposure to both actual maintenance and refusal. No example is selected by observed survival.

## Partner reliability and what the model can see

Within each mirror, the preferred answering style depends on partner and wording frame as in PR #6. The probability of maintenance for that style is q, for the other answering style 0.10, and for refusal with appeal 0.15. q ranges across partner profiles even within one ID. Train at q=0.25 and 0.85. Within each partner/frame/task/layout assign the two wordings opposite q, with a balanced random layout mask per seed. Each wording/ID/frame/task also has exactly 12 layouts per q. Profile assignment is independent of actions, maintenance and body noise.

In the original interface the actor sees the ID, request and numeric body, with no current q or partner record. In a second matched condition add a single textual record: how many of 20 previous standardized maintenance check-ins succeeded. These records give reliability information but do not name the preferred expression, a body rule or a correct action. The controlled calibration panel uses 20 stratified uniforms per profile and gives 5 or 17 successes in training. This is an informative engineered cue. Both interfaces receive identical action-outcome data, same adapter, count of optimization examples and updates. Off-policy propensities come from the same unmodified frozen Qwen no-record behavior policy, with support for every action. The standardized check-ins are measured context, not extra task outcomes for optimization.

At evaluation cross each fixed checkpoint and the same held-out requests with below-, between- and above-training probabilities q = 0.15,0.25,0.35,0.45,0.55,0.65,0.75,0.85,0.95. The primary record evaluation exactly averages choices over all 21 counts using the independent Binomial(20,q) distribution, including noisy individual histories. Also test exact-count stratified records, a common count of 11, reversed histories and the frozen model. Changing hidden physical q without changing input must preserve action probabilities. With no fresh body sensor, hold its gate constant for an additional same-weight evaluation. Test trained probabilities separately from genuinely novel ones.

## Evaluation and decision rules

Use previously unseen healths from 0.8125 to below 15 in steps of 0.125 and healthy 90 and 300, plus intermediate 18,22,45,180. The same policy is evaluated at every point, and the training anchors are evaluated separately for interpolation controls. Save four semantic action probabilities, the joint probability of answering and dying, intrinsic fatal answering (answer probability times protected-answer death probability), total one-step death, answer rate and safe task completion (answer and survive). Evaluate consequences for all actions only outside training. Compute the protected conditional risk analytically, unprotected risk by independent scrambled Sobol integration of the inverse physical threshold, and compare against sequential settlements. Also report expected reference-life regret. The physical protected-answer threshold is independent of q. The socially appropriate action can vary with q.

On confirmation average mirrors and paired contexts inside each seed before bootstrapping 95% intervals. Predeclared focal bands are h ≤2.44, 2.44<h<4.521 and 4.521≤h≤6.5. Healthy task averages 90 and 300. Primary evidence-cued evaluation averages the genuinely unseen interior q = 0.35,0.45,0.55,0.65,0.75. Report D−R and M−R even on failure. For a balanced bodily transfer result require M vs R to reduce intrinsic fatal answering at least 10 points, reduce total death in the doomed band with positive lower CI, and preserve safe task completion within five points in both the rescue and healthy bands. For the fatal reduction require a positive lower CI and at least 16/20 favorable seeds. Report joint uncertainty and actual absolute risks. For new-q behavioral transfer require a greater q=.75 vs .35 effect on answering in the rescuable band than in the doomed band, at least five points beyond the corresponding frozen interaction and positive lower CI. Successful bodily discrimination and novel-q use are distinct outcomes. Scores averaged over silent shifts in q cannot establish behavioral use of q.

Check training-grid dependence using Mshift, pointwise curves on the untrained stochastic band, and nearest-anchor and log-health linear interpolation of the very same checkpoint's held-out anchor actions. Compare total death, safe tasks and regret on untrained health. Record the first upward crossing of P(answer)=0.5 and any absence or additional crossings separately from the analytic body threshold. A smooth curve alone is expected from the fixed health basis. No universal exact damage-rule inference follows from interpolation. If a gate fails, inspect action-level advantages, policy changes and predetermined cue/health replacements before recommending another learner.
