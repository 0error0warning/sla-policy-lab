# Put the extra damage penalty only in low-health encounters

Protocol locked before new model outputs. The parent is PR #11, 965c16a. The intervention is determined at the beginning of a living encounter. Use k=1 for pre-action health h>6, k=2 for 0<h≤6. Use the same k for all four actions and either maintenance response. The crossing encounter that starts above 6 still uses 1. This includes the previously defined survivable practice band FATAL<h≤6 and later first-fatal/terminal encounters. Terminal feedback may update the final saved parameters and has no next decision. H≥90 is the original healthy task endpoint. The middle band uses 1. Do not scan thresholds or tune on the pilot.

Use the same Qwen3.5-4B revision, terminal private LoRA, visible health channel, physical body, partner rules and action layouts. D=RUL(h)−RUL(after), b is the mean of the individual's own earlier *unweighted* negative costs at pre-action health in [h/1.25,1.25h]. Fewer than three such observations uses the original global fallback with eight observations of −1. Training signal = −k(h)D−b. Learning rate 0.18, gradient normalization, clipping, exploration and the feedback schedule follow PR #11. Cost is only a training coefficient. The full 1× and full 2× always use their own constant coefficient.

Report all living successors, previously healthy answers, intermediate-state transitions, all low-health injuries and the 660 archived survivable risky answering injuries. ## New independent lives

Use pilot seeds 72101–72108 and an independent replication 72201–72216. Each seed receives two births (no prior body training or population-trained), seven separately acting arms at each birth: frozen; constant 1×, conditional and constant 2× each with correct and mismatched action credit. This assigns 336 distinct private lives on 24 independent environmental seeds. Run all waves regardless of pilot signs. Match arrivals and uniforms by seed and time; each body acts autonomously until natural death. Wrong credit uses the same actual scalar and the parent's pre-outcome nonzero label offset. Audit the healthy and pre-switch trajectories of conditional versus 1× under both credit assignments. Identical real healthy behavior on these pairs is expected by construction. Compare actual healthy task completion against frozen as a separate test.

On each conditional-correct life also run three non-acting adapters on its exact actions and observations: conditional with wrong credit, correct always 1×, correct always 2×. On the first living request where either answer is fatal even if maintained, score before its feedback with own birth, deployed, conditional wrong, matched 1×, matched 2× and a deployed duplicate. Retain the entry checkpoint separately. Constant correct lives keep their parent's same-history diagnostics. Read-only probes use the actual request, the same request at healthy 90/300, and the 96 balanced held-out task contexts (2 partners × 2 frames × 24 layouts) across all three health values. Independently acting arms can reach a different first request. Report actual drawn first survival for every arm with paired discordances and competing deaths. Healthy task completion is a surviving answer at h≥90 while actually alive. At the final low-health checkpoint also assess hypothetical healthy tasks on fixed held-out texts. Health never resets in the live trajectories.

## Decision rule and repeatability

For each birth, use seven primary comparisons on the new seeds. For lower-risk superiority compare conditional-correct minus birth, matched conditional wrong and matched 1× on the actual gated individual's first-fatal input. Include matched 2× minus birth as a positive control for whether a benefit exists to retain on those same lives. For these four contrasts require adjusted upper bound below 0 and negative means on actual and balanced held-out danger inputs in *each* independent wave. For risk retention compare conditional minus matched 2×: adjusted upper bound below +0.02, with actual and held-out differences below +0.02 per wave. The two-point tolerance is an absolute, prechosen noninferiority margin, not a fitted estimate. For healthy task retention require adjusted lower bounds above −0.02 on (a) held-out healthy conditional versus its own birth at the first-fatal checkpoint and (b) actually completed healthy tasks versus the independently acting paired frozen control. Also report healthy differences versus both free constant policies without conflating guaranteed prefix equality with acquired competence. Full joint success needs all seven components, all assigned conditional subjects reaching first-fatal, and no numerical/chronological validation failure. Report individual components even when the intersection fails.

Use 50,000 paired bootstrap resamples with the whole independent environment seed as the unit, stratified by pilot and replication, one-sided alpha 0.05/14 across both births and seven comparisons. Show ordinary 95% intervals too. Single uniforms and multiple actions in a life are not additional independent seeds. Retain on-device full Qwen versus frozen-prefix score and gradient checks; fall back to ordinary forwards on mismatches. Reinstall the primary snapshots in a fresh model and ordinary from-token forwards, including both constant shadows on the conditional lives. Technical repeats do not contribute new seeds. Archive the pinned input and output transports, source provenance, ancestral and new full trajectories, CPU audit, seed-level tables, numerical repeats and provider releases.
