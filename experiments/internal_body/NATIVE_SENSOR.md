# Small original-parameter follow-up

After the four-seed initial pilot, native final-norm-only training with a health prompt had approximately a one-point healthy-minus-critical answering difference. Test a specific mechanism with unchanged trainable parameter identity. Train only the original 2,560 RMSNorm scale coordinates on the same actual actions, body settlements, 75 updates, rate, KL and reference-life credit. Compare a numeric prompt, hidden health and a fixed explicit physical signal at the last decoder residual.

The physical signal rotates the native residual in a plane orthogonal to the four original effective action-output directions. The first axis is the unlabeled training mean after projecting away those four directions. The second is a smooth interpolation of twelve seed-random orthonormal axes in the same complement. Rotate by 60 degrees. Both the native residual length and its four action projections are preserved at birth in real arithmetic. It is deliberately engineered for initial four-action neutrality. It can alter other vocabulary logits; quantify collateral changes. No action-body rule, partner routing, rewarding action or ecology labels go into this rotation. The coordinates of the single original scale parameter are the only learned values. Save the fixed basis, master and effective weights, and test with an exchanged random carrier. Record BF16 full-forward error and exact native-parameter change.

Four independent pilots 64401–64404; twenty new confirmation seeds 64501–64520 if the pilot gate passes. Each seed draws its own carrier basis independently of its feedback. If mean critical answering is >=10 points lower than both frozen and carrier/shuffle, healthy answering is within a five-point loss, and the healthy-minus-critical gap exceeds hidden-native by >=10 points, advance; require >=3/4 favorable for the critical reduction. In confirmation also require >=16/20 favorable clusters and positive lower paired bootstrap bounds for critical reductions and the gap. Separately report style, total death risk, and whether the putative bodily gain generalizes to mixed social settings. These gates concern body switching, not proficiency at personalized language.

An ancillary frozen-state diagnostic estimates a low/high text-health direction from paired training requests (health 1.25/2.5 versus 120/350) and evaluates the direction at different healths on held-out requests (2/3 versus 90/300). Report between-context classification and within-identical-text ranking, shuffled train labels, prompt length cue and hidden-text negative control.
