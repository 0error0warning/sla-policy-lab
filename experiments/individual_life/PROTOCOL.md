# Learning during one finite life

Parent bba1274 (PR #7). The primary unit is one newborn agent, its own body, its own parameter copy and the requests it actually receives until it dies. Population training is used only as an optional birth initialization. No personal optimizer, experience, baseline, gradient or learned weight passes from one life to another. Evaluation seeds replicate whole lives. This protocol is fixed before observing pilot outcomes.

## Birth, interaction and credit

Use the same pinned Qwen3.5-4B, final decoder block 31, 13,408 rank-four body-conditioned A/B coordinates, four original action tokens, temperature 2 and 5% exploration. The blank birth has the original deterministic unlabeled A initialization and B=0. The population birth uses PR #6 generic-64001.npz trained on the two pooled response ecologies. For both starts initialize the two new partner A slots from their mean so that neither new ID receives an assigned social history. B is copied without modification. The blank model retains ordinary Qwen pretraining and the same engineered body/identity interface. No new population fitting is allowed.

Each birth starts at health 350. New identities P86 and P93 have stationary, unknown response tendencies. The underlying favorite answer style depends on the ID and request framing. Giving the favored answering style receives maintenance with probability 0.9, the other 0.1. Refusal with appeal has probability 0.15. Silent refusal has no partner response. The two hidden partner assignments are balanced over seeds, including worlds in which the two partners have the same or opposite tastes. A block of eight requests contains two of each identity/frame cell, and every 24 displays cover all option orders. Independently keyed uniforms drive action sampling, partner response and the five bodily noise draws. They are paired across study conditions. An untouched task and wording are reserved for read-only tests. The agent observes its request and ID. The visible condition also receives current health in the original numeric gate, with no written health, partner success record or conversation transcript.

Per actual encounter Qwen samples its own action, then action damage and four environmental events settle. Maintenance blocks only the current environmental events. Health does not reset. Reward is the observed change in reference remaining life, with no separate maintenance bonus or synchronous oracle labels. The optimizer sees only this one realized scalar. Center it using a personal baseline initialized with eight pseudo-observations of -1, then all earlier rewards of this individual. Use chosen-action log policy gradient with both A and B trainable. The Euclidean update is 0.18 × centered reward × the clipped importance ratio × the score gradient, divided by 0.01 plus squared gradient norm, with a cap of 0.40 on step norm. No momentum or weight decay. The importance ratio is the training-time probability of the credited action divided by its logged behavior probability, clipped to [0.5,2]. Score, gradient, baseline, ratio, norms and actual before/after parameter digests are recorded. The environment's response probability and its four counterfactual action values are used only by the evaluator.

## Paired conditions

For each origin separately run frozen, correctly credited every-turn, deliberately wrong credit every-turn, correctly credited every eight, and shuffled credit every eight. The immediate wrong-credit arm applies precisely the actual feedback to a different action in the same context, chosen by a pre-outcome nonzero cyclic offset. This changes credited action labels. In the delayed wrong-credit arm permute centered feedback within each completed block, keep the executed actions and states, and preserve the exact marginal signal values. This control never reads a future event before deployment. The block contains eight observations, each getting one chronological SGD microstep when the block closes. The one-step reward horizon is identical across schedules. Final partial blocks flush at death and are marked as unusable by any later living decision. A block of one cannot be permuted. No artificial length cutoff is needed: every live turn loses at least 0.175 health until the last clipped transition.

Each correctly credited live trajectory carries an independent same-birth shadow that gets its identical observed requests, actions, partner responses and scalar feedback with the corresponding wrong assignment. It never selects the source's actions. Also retain the every-turn source's parameter snapshot at the last complete eight-event block: with the same chronological microsteps and logged propensities, this is the delayed deployment on that fixed tape. Verify boundary equality by independently replaying recorded data. The separately acting every-eight and every-turn lives may diverge, so their realized exposure counts and numbers of alive opportunities are reported.

## What constitutes an effect

At the actual next request while the body is alive, score the same text and same current body using (1) parameters that chose the preceding action, (2) parameters after its credited update, (3) the individual's original birth, (4) the matched wrong-credit shadow where applicable, (5) the last complete eight-turn snapshot on the true-every-turn tape, and (6) a duplicate of current for numerical checks. Sample the actual action only from current. Save all four semantic probabilities, common-uniform counterfactual choices, physical before/after details, utterance, reward, source turns and exact update ledger. Terminal updates have no next-choice observation. The primary same-state contrasts are one-update change and cumulative change versus own birth and matched wrong credit. Compute observed risk, safe tasks and expected reference-life value for each distribution. For a comparable per-state unit, scale expected reference value by the attainable max-minus-min of the four physical action values, assigning zero contrast to a tie. Report raw reference-life units alongside this scaled measure. Aggregate living transitions within the individual before aggregating across the independent seeds.

Pilot 68011–68014, four independent environments per condition. Run the visible stage first. After its chronology, state isolation and no-update checks pass, use the same four seeds for a separate hidden-health pilot (frozen, true and wrong every-turn for each origin). In the hidden actor the body gate is constant during both action and learning. The simulator still delivers its observed scalar after settlement. At identical text and parameter snapshot the hidden outputs must be invariant to instantaneous health. Score matched held-out probes at health 2, 6, 90 and 300 at birth and after 8, 16, 32 and 48 turns only if alive. A missing late probe is reported as missing. Also probe the first living arrival at health at most 10. Never count a terminal snapshot as evidence of a later decision.

The ordinary complete Qwen physically acts and updates its installed A/B by autograd for all four pilot seeds in the visible frozen, true1 and wrong1 arms, for both origins. Additionally run the first seed's two block conditions and hidden immediate conditions from birth to death on the complete model. The same protocol is evaluated for all pilot cells with real immutable upstream Qwen tensors and the live native suffix. Frozen prefixes contain no outcomes, futures or personal parameters. Distinguish separate executions from new independent environments.

Predefined expansion criterion for an origin: on the true immediate complete-model trajectories let G be the mean attained-value difference versus own birth at later living decisions, C the difference versus its same-observations wrong-credit shadow, and L the one-update change versus the pre-update snapshot on that next state. Require G and C both at least 0.02 in the same direction, at least three of four seeds with both differences in that direction, and positive directional L in at least three of four seeds. A coherent negative direction qualifies for a harm replication and cannot be called improvement. Require the cached pilot signs for G and C to agree. If qualified, use twelve locked, disjoint seeds 68101–68112 for the qualifying origin's visible frozen/true1/wrong1 lives and report independent seed uncertainty. If neither qualifies, stop increasing seeds; diagnose experience coverage, feedback assignment, timing and the difference between probability movement and useful decisions on these same lives. Separately assess dangerous and healthy choices, task counts, life length and untrained-wording probes. An improvement in social choice or marginal reward is not by itself evidence that an individual acquired a body-sensitive response from scratch.
