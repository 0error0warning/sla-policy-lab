# Prospective personal-learning and complete-life follow-up

Locked after the four-seed population pilot and before any complete-life results. Confirmatory seed IDs for the population fits stay unchanged. No additional population training.

All agents start at health 350. Death is absorbing. The administrative cap is 256. Each eight-turn block crosses two known partners and two request styles twice; their order is sampled. All 24 action layouts occur in randomized cycles. Only test task 4 is encountered. Test task 5 is reserved for post-life probes at identical health, text and partner, with the chat history absent. The two partners' personal preference maps vary independently, using all four combinations and two new exogenous streams per combination and training seed. Each complete life uses only its own outcomes. Noise and sampling uniforms are paired by encounter index across arms, with trajectories allowed to diverge. Confirmatory uncertainty resamples 20 independent training and environment seed clusters; there are eight lives per cluster per condition.

Two separately trained population adapters from opposite acquisition ecologies provide a prior mean and a learned contrast between them. The personal learner starts at the mean. Two zero-initialized scalar parameters, one per observed partner ID, scale that contrast in Qwen's native output head. They can be folded into its coefficient bank. This is an explicitly provided partner-specific learning subspace. The policy never receives the current ecology, request-frame label, response probabilities, best action, or action values. Each settled encounter updates its current partner scalar using the gradient of the actual sampled action's log probability, the realized reference-life change divided by min(1, pre-action reference life), a fixed action-independent baseline of -0.9 in these units and an expected-score normalization (1 + Fisher information). Learning rate 0.5, coefficients clipped to [-1,1]. The four ecology combinations test whether two different partners can acquire different parameters. Two combinations were absent as whole worlds from population acquisition.

Compare (a) the same prior kept fixed, (b) frozen original output, (c) matched population and shuffled-feedback checkpoints, (d) a fresh zero-initialized adapter updated in one life without the learned contrast, and (e) the personal learner. The matched population condition is a deployment benchmark initialized with the fitting population for each partner. It uses information unavailable to the personal learner. A tape control replays the personal learner's actual actions and logged score sensitivities, shuffling normalized outcomes within partner and broad health band. Replaying the original correspondence must exactly recover the saved personal scalars; both replay conditions start from zero. These tape controls are post-hoc sensitivity interventions and do not generate lives.

The preregistered personal-learning endpoint is preferred-style answering on the reserved task at health 90/300 after the life. Require at least 10 percentage points over both the identical fixed prior and permuted replay, with at least 16/20 favorable seed means and positive lower paired 95% bootstrap bounds. Also report both partner sign recovery, effect on low-health answering, complete lifetime, tasks completed, near-death task choices and censoring. Benefits at terminal health can originate in the population prior. Record rather than require complete-life extension.
