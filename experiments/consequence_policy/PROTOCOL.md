# Consequence-conditioned native Qwen action policy

Locked before observing any new model outputs on 12 November 2026 (Taipei). Parent is ca8e141. The previous experiments and negative results are preserved separately in commit 7801984.

## Scientific contrast

Each action comes from Qwen3.5-4B's categorical next-token distribution over four stable native output words. The original LM head is augmented only at those four vocabulary rows. The residual is linear in an unlabeled 32-dimensional projection of its final hidden state, uses a known partner identity and interpolates learned coefficients using 12 smooth functions of measured log-health. The output table is permuted through all 24 orders. No health number, explicit request-frame category, partner probabilities, action labels, or history enter the text. This is a deliberately provided bodily sensor and action interface. The body gate contains no action values. The experiment tests whether realized consequences train a conditional policy in this architecture. It cannot by itself show that the original Qwen backbone discovers a bodily representation.

Use the inherited action cost, fragility, truncated noise, four aging events, maintenance timing and absorbing death. Sum no task bonus. The scalar reward for the selected action is the difference in numerical reference remaining life before and after settlement (reference continuation always silently declines unprotected). Use the same prior numerical quadrature as the lab baseline. The two social ecologies prefer opposite styles for each partner and request frame. The partner acts on the chosen language category. Content is rendered by existing templates; completion means choosing an answering act.

## Acquisition and fitting

Data come from independent single-encounter replicas at eight healths from 1.25 through 350. For each of 768 training prompts, eight healths and eight replicas, sample a single action from the frozen Qwen behavior policy mixed with 55% uniform exploration. Keep actual maintenance, damage and death. Each training seed supplies 49,152 choices per ecology. Same sampled choices, random uniforms and bodily noises across mirrored ecologies. Arms within an ecology share the identical tape. Train only the output adapter with an importance-weighted categorical policy objective, eight-replica leave-one-out reward baseline and a state-only reward scale min(1, reference life before). Penalize KL to the frozen policy. Test on a different wording and two new tasks in each request frame, all orders, both partners, eight untrained healths. Inference uses temperature 2 and 5% uniform exploration. The blind adapter shares one health coefficient bank. Shuffle realized returns within exact prompt, health and seed blocks; preserve marginal feedback. Disabling the adapter recovers the frozen policy.

## Prospective decision

Pilot 63001–63004, then disjoint confirmation seeds 63101–63120 in both mirror ecologies. Average mirrors, prompts and orders within each seed. At health 2, answering is certainly fatal under the bounded noise. At health 90 and 300, report actual answering acts, preferred protected answering style, and separately evaluate complete lives from 350 to death or 256 encounters. At health 3, 4, 6, 10 and 18 report graded transfer. Primary pilot and confirmation gates for the true-feedback arm are (i) at least 10 percentage points less fatal answering at health 2 versus each of frozen and shuffled, (ii) mean healthy answering no more than 5 points below frozen, (iii) conditional healthy-minus-danger answering gap at least 10 points larger than blind, and (iv) at least 10 points more correctly styled healthy answers than shuffled. For confirmation require the mean gates and favorable direction on (i), (iii), (iv) on at least 16 of 20 independent seeds, plus a positive lower 95% paired seed-bootstrap bound on those differences. Show all outcomes and changes in both regimes. Mirrored worlds and turns are paired observations, not independent replications. A single simulator family and two known partner IDs limit transfer.

Use the same training algorithm and fixed hyperparameters through the confirmatory wave. If pilot fails, retain it and preregister any revision with untouched seeds. An independent GPU process checks installed full-vocabulary Qwen action logits against the frozen-prefix implementation and deploys selected complete lives. Population training on checkpoint replicas and improvement from the experiences of a single continuous lifetime are separate endpoints. Test complete lives, including personal updates and frozen-personal controls, separately. Keep failures, checkpoints, actual trajectories, seed-level results, hashes and spent compute.
